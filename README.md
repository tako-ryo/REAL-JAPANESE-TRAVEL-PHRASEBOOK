# Real Japanese Travel Phrasebook for Beginners

*83 Survival Phrases for Your First Trip to Japan*

Read it here: **https://tako-ryo.github.io/REAL-JAPANESE-TRAVEL-PHRASEBOOK/**

For an English speaker with no Japanese who is flying to Japan soon.
Every Japanese line in the book has its sounds printed beside it, so
nothing here needs to be studied before it can be used.

## What is in it

- 83 phrases, one to a page, grouped by where you will be standing:
  anywhere, the airport, getting around, restaurants, shopping, the
  hotel, and when it goes wrong
- A survival kit before the phrases start — numbers, money, counters,
  the clock, the signs you have to read, the ticket machine, the
  convenience-store register, allergies
- Seven quizzes with an answer key, three roleplay scripts, an index,
  and kana sheets for the menu the book does not print

158 pages, 6 × 9in.

## The file

`index.html` is the whole book on one page, with the illustrations
embedded. The `media` folder holds the Japanese typeface it needs; keep
the two together.

To print it, open it in a browser and use Save as PDF at 6 × 9in with
background graphics turned on.

## Tako-sensei Series

One of five books that teach the Japanese people actually speak. The
others cover the grammar underneath, anime and everyday expressions,
